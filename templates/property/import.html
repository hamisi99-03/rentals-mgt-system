Before you import

Every row is validated first. If any row has an error, nothing is committed.

  • The template sheets are Buildings, Floors, Units, Tenants and Leases. Leave a sheet empty if you do not need it.
  • Existing building, floor and tenant names are reused. Unit codes and tenant business names must not already exist.
  • Only one active lease may overlap a unit. Occupied status is derived from leases, never imported directly.
  • Opening balances are imported after the billing phase, when invoices and credit entries exist.
  • Want demo data after setup? Download the sample 40-unit workbook above, then upload it below. It creates 2 buildings, 4 floors, 40 units, 40 tenants and 40 active leases.
